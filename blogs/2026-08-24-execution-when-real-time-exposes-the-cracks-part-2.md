At the moment a vessel approaches the berth, fragmented data exchange becomes impossible to ignore. Arrival times shift, berths are reassigned, crane availability fluctuates—yet stakeholders rarely see the same version of reality. "Everyone knows there's a problem, but no one sees the same version of it." The Kaleris Cargo Data Exchange Hub changes this—enabling direct, real-time data exchange between ship and shore.
