<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Problem: Manual Processes Create Delays, Blind Spots, and Operational Strain</strong></h2>



<p>From a transportation manager’s perspective, manual yard processes quickly become a bottleneck, especially in large, high-traffic facilities.</p>



<p>Without a centralized, real-time system:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Yard teams waste <strong>15–30 minutes per trailer</strong> searching for equipment, multiplying into hours of lost productivity each day.</li>



<li>Clerks, spotters, supervisors, and drivers operate without a shared source of truth, increasing miscommunication and errors.</li>



<li>FIFO workflows are difficult to enforce, leading to trailers sitting too long and slowing downstream processing.</li>



<li>Managers lack instant access to accurate inventory data, making it harder to plan labor, prioritize moves, and meet service-level expectations.</li>



<li>Reporting becomes reactive instead of strategic, requiring manual checks rather than real-time insights.</li>
</ul>



<p>In short, transportation managers are forced to spend valuable time tracking assets instead of managing performance, improving flow, and planning the next move.</p>
