Terminal yards are constantly in flux - container flows shift, vessel schedules change, and density rises and falls throughout the day. Traditional planning approaches ask planners to manually monitor every change and adjust strategies accordingly, adding significant workload to teams already stretched thin. But what if the system could adapt to changing conditions while keeping the planner in control?
