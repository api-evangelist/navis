<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-color" style="color: #000000; font-size: 32px;"><strong><strong><strong><strong>N4 4.0 : Strengthening the Foundation</strong></strong></strong></strong></h2>



<p class="has-text-color" style="color: #000000; font-size: 17px;">As terminals scale automation and optimization, a stable and reliable core is essential. <strong>N4 4.0 delivers a major step forward in reliability, performance, and stability</strong>, with many customers already upgraded. The impact is tangible. The world’s first fully automated terminal<strong>, </strong><a href="https://www.apmterminals.com/en/maasvlakte/about/news-and-updates/2025/250404-new-terminal-operating-system-launched"><strong>MVII, Rotterdam</strong></a> upgraded to N4 4.0 in early 2025 laying the groundwork for their Automated Terminal Trucks and advanced rail automation. At <a href="https://kaleris.com/news/ptp-goes-live-with-navis-n4-4-0/">Port of Tanjung Pelepas (PTP)</a>, Malaysia the move to N4 4.0 delivered immediate productivity gains with no patches, no downtime, and no disruption, highlighting the platform’s maturity and robustness. Key enhancements such as Kafka-based messaging and improved configuration have significantly improved speed, uptime, and stability.</p>
