Car carrier stowage planning defies full automation. Unlike container shipping with its standardized slots, RoRo vessels demand human expertise to manage thousands of unique vehicles across multi-deck layouts, shifting port rotations, and real-time disruptions. Software handles calculations—but judgment, adaptability, and accountability remain irreplaceable.
