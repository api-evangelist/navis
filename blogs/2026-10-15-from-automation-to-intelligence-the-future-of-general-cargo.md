The next generation of terminal technology won't be defined by how much of the operation can be automated. It will be defined by how intelligently the operation can respond to change. Drawing on the Kaleris webinar Turning Innovation into Operational Value with Kaleris General Cargo TOS, this blog looks at configurable workflows, open integrations and AI-assisted work order management.
