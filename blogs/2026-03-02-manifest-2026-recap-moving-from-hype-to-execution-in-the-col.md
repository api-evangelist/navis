<p style="font-size: 18px;">Another Manifest is in the books, and after three days in Las Vegas connecting with over 7,200 of the industry&#8217;s brightest minds,&nbsp;I’m&nbsp;coming back to the office energized.&nbsp;</p>



<p style="font-size: 18px;">If&nbsp;you’ve&nbsp;been to Manifest in previous years, you know the vibe has&nbsp;often heavily&nbsp;favored visionary futurism. But this year felt different.&nbsp;Walking&nbsp;the floor and sitting in on sessions, the tone has clearly shifted from &#8220;what if&#8221; to &#8220;what now.&#8221; The conversations our team had with shippers, carriers, and retailers&nbsp;weren&#8217;t about&nbsp;experimental pilots; they were about budget-ready solutions that solve immediate, messy, real-world problems.&nbsp;</p>



<p style="font-size: 18px;">For us at&nbsp;Kaleris, it was an&nbsp;incredibly&nbsp;fulfilling week. The industry is converging exactly where we have been building. Here are my top three takeaways from the event and what they mean for our customers in&nbsp;2026.&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. The Cold Chain is No Longer a Side Conversation&nbsp;</h2>



<p>One of the most significant shifts this year was the spotlight on temperature-controlled&nbsp;logistics.&nbsp;It&nbsp;wasn&#8217;t&nbsp;just a niche topic tucked away in a corner; it was a main event, complete with a dedicated pavilion and stage.&nbsp;</p>



<p>This renewed focus made our conversations at the booth incredibly productive. It is exactly why we brought&nbsp;<a href="https://kaleris.com/cold-link/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Kaleris&nbsp;ColdLink</a>&nbsp;to market.&nbsp;</p>



<p>The feedback we received on the floor was clear: shippers are tired of trying to force-fit generalist platforms into specialized cold chain workflows. They need purpose-built technology that ensures product integrity and compliance from the moment the door closes to the final delivery. Seeing the industry embrace this need confirms that our focus on deep, specialized visibility for refrigerated assets is hitting the mark at the exact right time.&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list" style="font-size: 18px;">
<li><strong>Manual reconciliation cycles that never end</strong></li>



<li><strong>Inconsistent units and fuel naming across vessels and fleets</strong></li>



<li><strong>“Why did this number change?” debates before every submission</strong></li>



<li><strong>Last-minute surprises that become last-minute cost</strong></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. &#8220;Agentic AI&#8221; is Replacing Generative Hype&nbsp;</h2>



<p>For the past two years, the industry buzz has been dominated by Generative AI. However, the discussions at Manifest 2026 moved firmly toward&nbsp;<strong>Agentic AI</strong>.&nbsp;</p>



<p>We saw a clear trend of software providers pivoting away from passive bots that offer advice, moving toward autonomous agents that execute tasks. This&nbsp;tracks with&nbsp;recent market data from ABI Research, which found that&nbsp;<strong>76% of supply chain professionals now see the potential for autonomous AI agents</strong>&nbsp;to handle execution tasks like reordering and shipment rerouting [1].&nbsp;</p>



<p style="font-size: 18px;">As a Product Marketer, I find this shift exciting because it aligns perfectly with our goal of delivering&nbsp;<em>actionable</em>&nbsp;intelligence. The industry is done playing with AI; they are ready to let it handle the manual drudgery of execution.&nbsp;</p>



<p style="font-size: 18px;">This is the exact philosophy driving the AI routing capabilities within&nbsp;our&nbsp;<a href="https://kaleris.com/transportation-management/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Kaleris&nbsp;TMS</a>. Instead of just passively analyzing historical transit times or suggesting a route, our TMS leverages AI to autonomously evaluate real-time network conditions, carrier capacities, and dynamic constraints to&nbsp;actually execute&nbsp;the most efficient, cost-effective routing decisions. By automating the heavy lifting of complex freight planning, we free up your human teams to step out of the tactical weeds and focus on overarching strategy.&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Security Meets the Digital Chain of Custody&nbsp;</h2>



<p>Security was a dominant theme, and for good reason. According to the year-end 2025 report from Verisk&nbsp;CargoNet, while overall theft incident volume remained&nbsp;relatively stable,&nbsp;<strong>the total estimated value of stolen cargo surged by 60%, reaching&nbsp;nearly $725 million</strong>&nbsp;[2]. Furthermore, the average value per theft rose 36% to&nbsp;nearly $274,000. Thieves are getting smarter, targeting higher-value goods with more precision.&nbsp;</p>



<p style="font-size: 18px;">The solution&nbsp;isn&#8217;t&nbsp;just about stronger&nbsp;hardware anymore—it&#8217;s&nbsp;about connectivity and strict perimeter control. We&nbsp;observed&nbsp;a wave of innovation where physical asset manufacturers are bridging the gap with software to create a &#8220;digital chain of custody.&#8221;&nbsp;</p>



<p style="font-size: 18px;">But securing the asset on the road is only half the battle; securing the facility is where execution software truly shines. This is exactly where&nbsp;<a href="https://kaleris.com/solutions/yard-management/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><strong>Kaleris&nbsp;YMS</strong></a>&nbsp;steps in to close the vulnerability gap and prevent unauthorized access. We protect against sophisticated freight fraud by automating perimeter defenses:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Controlled Appointment Scheduling:</strong>&nbsp;By enforcing strict, pre-approved arrival windows, the system ensures only expected carriers approach the facility, instantly flagging suspicious or unannounced arrivals.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>OCR at the Gate:</strong>&nbsp;Optical Character Recognition (OCR) automatically captures and verifies trailer, container, and license plate numbers. This&nbsp;eliminates&nbsp;manual data entry errors and stops bad actors from slipping past the guard shack with spoofed paperwork.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Double-Blind Verification:</strong>&nbsp;We&nbsp;eliminate&nbsp;the risk of verbal confirmation by requiring drivers to provide specific reference numbers that must perfectly match the secure, pre-loaded data in the YMS before access is ever granted.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<p style="font-size: 18px;">It’s&nbsp;not enough to&nbsp;lock&nbsp;a trailer; the facility itself must act as a digital fortress. This integration of physical security with digital execution data is the only way to combat the rising cost of freight crime.&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Final Thoughts&nbsp;</h2>



<p>We left Las Vegas with a confirmed sense of purpose. The industry is maturing, and the tolerance for disconnected, &#8220;hype-first&#8221; tech is lower than ever.&nbsp;</p>



<p>The themes of Manifest 2026—specialized execution, autonomous action, and connected&nbsp;security—are the pillars we are building on. It was great to show the world how&nbsp;Kaleris&nbsp;ColdLink, TMS, and YMS fit into this new era, and we are ready to help our customers turn these takeaways into operational reality this year.&nbsp;</p>
